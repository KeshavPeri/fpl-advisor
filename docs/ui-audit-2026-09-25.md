## Global
- G1 No screen titles. Every screen opens straight into a card, so nothing anchors you. Add a real title per screen.
- G2 Two card styles (blue glass vs near-black) used with no meaning. Pick one, plus one emphasis variant.
- G3 Monospace used at random: prices, countdown, deadline, chip statuses ("Available", "Locked"),
  "MAE". Keep mono only for tabular figures that need it; statuses and dates go sans.
- G4 Boilerplate filler: "X — built on real Premier League match history." under every player on
  Reasoning. It says nothing. Replace with the gbm-v1 top reasons (#266) or remove.
- G5 Repeated information: the same sentence as heading and again as the first bullet;
  "coin-flip" stated 2–3 times per card.
- G6 Hedging prose inside cards ("This is a five-gameweek view — a wildcard or free hit's real value
  depends on fixtures the model cannot see", "The solver only sees five gameweeks ahead, so…"). At most
  one short caption, or an info tooltip.
- G7 Jargon on screen: "MAE", "non-appearances", "baseline-v1", "1 of 3 solutions".
- G8 When scrolled, the glass header blob sits under the status bar and looks like a glitch.

### Mini-league — direction
- Big rank as the hero with a movement chip (▲2 / ▼1 / –). Points count up on load.
- A "gap" visual: a progress bar or ladder to the place above ("12 pts to 1st"), or the lead over 2nd when first.
- Show top 3 + you ± 1 (not just 2 rows). Team name large, manager name small, your row glowing.
- One tasteful moment of delight when leading (subtle crown or glow). No confetti.

### Reasoning ("Why") — direction: one screen you can read in 10 seconds
- Hero: the plan in one line ("Rogers in, Wirtz out · Captain Haaland") + one confidence badge
  ("Close call" instead of "coin-flip" repeated 4×).
- Per player: name, team, **"5.6 pts next gameweek"**, and up to 3 plain-English reason chips built
  from the driver *values*, not feature names: "Played 90 mins last game", "Managers are buying him",
  "Top-priced, nailed starter", "Easy fixture". Never "pushes up / pulls down", never "gbm-v1".
- Remove "— built on real Premier League match history" everywhere.
- The 8-row points breakdown goes behind a "See the numbers" disclosure. "Alternatives considered"
  becomes a collapsed "Other options" list: one line each ("Schade instead of Rogers · same points").
- No model names, "MAE", "explainable model", "projection" on this screen. The accuracy card moves off
  it, or becomes a single plain line.
